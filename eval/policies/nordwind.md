# Nordwind Outdoor Privacy Notice

## About us

This website is run by Nordwind Outdoor AB, Storgatan 8, 111 51 Stockholm, Sweden. Questions about this notice can be sent to hello@nordwind.example.

## Information we use

When you place an order we use your name, delivery address, phone number and payment details to process your order, deliver it and handle returns.

If you tick the box at checkout, you agree to receive our newsletter with offers.

We also record which pages you visit on our website to improve our product range.

## Sharing

We share your name, address and phone number with our delivery partners so they can deliver your parcel. Payment details go directly to our payment provider. All of your data is stored and processed within the European Union; we do not transfer it to other countries.

## Retention

We keep order information for as long as we need it to handle returns, warranty claims and our accounting obligations, and then delete it. Newsletter subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe. Page-visit records are deleted after 13 months.

## Questions and complaints

If you have any questions about your data, just contact us at hello@nordwind.example. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) or to the supervisory authority in your own country.
