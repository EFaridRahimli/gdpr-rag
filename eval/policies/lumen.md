# Lumen Talent Candidate Privacy Policy

## Who is responsible

Lumen Talent B.V., Herengracht 210, 1016 BS Amsterdam, the Netherlands, is the controller of candidate data processed on the Lumen platform. Contact us at privacy@lumen.example. Our data protection officer is reachable at dpo@lumen.example.

## Why we process candidate data

We process candidate profiles, including names, work history, skills and contact details, to match candidates with vacancies posted by employers on our platform and to let employers contact suitable candidates.

We process this data on the basis of our legitimate interests.

## Matching

Our software automatically scores and ranks candidates for each vacancy. Employers only see the candidates our software ranks highest.

## Who receives candidate data

Employers who use the Lumen platform receive the profiles of candidates matched to their vacancies. Our cloud hosting provider stores the data in data centres in the Netherlands and Germany.

## How long we keep candidate data

We delete a candidate profile two years after the last time it was matched to a vacancy, unless the candidate asks us to delete it earlier.

## Your rights

You can ask us for access to your data, ask us to correct or erase it, restrict or object to our processing, and request a copy in a machine-readable format. Email privacy@lumen.example. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
