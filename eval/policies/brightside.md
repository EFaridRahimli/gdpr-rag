# Brightside Fitness Privacy Policy

## Who we are

Brightside Fitness GmbH, Torstraße 140, 10119 Berlin, Germany, is the controller responsible for your personal data. You can reach us at privacy@brightside.example or by post at the address above. Our data protection officer can be contacted at dpo@brightside.example.

## What we collect and why

When you create an account we collect your name, email address, date of birth and password. We use this data to provide the Brightside app and your training plans, which is necessary to perform our contract with you.

If you connect a heart-rate monitor, we process your heart-rate and workout data to show your progress. We only do this with your explicit consent, which you give in the app settings.

With your consent we also send you a monthly newsletter about new workouts.

You must provide your name, email address and date of birth to create an account; without them we cannot provide the app. Everything else is optional.

## Who receives your data

We use a hosting provider to run our servers and a payment provider to process subscriptions; both are necessary to perform our contract with you. Both act on our instructions only. Our hosting provider stores backups on servers in the United States.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account is active and delete it within 30 days after you close your account. Payment records are kept for 10 years because German tax law requires it; keeping them is necessary to comply with that legal obligation.

## Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive your data in a portable format. Write to privacy@brightside.example to use these rights.

You can withdraw your consent to heart-rate processing or to the newsletter at any time in the app settings. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before.

## Automated decisions

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing.
